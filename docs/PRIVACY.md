# APrompter — Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2 October 2026_

APrompter is a teleprompter app. This policy explains what it does with your data.
Short version: **everything stays on your phone. We don't collect, see or sell anything.**

## What the app stores, and where

| Data | Where it lives | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Your scripts, version history, "Recently deleted" | Inside the app on your phone | You |
| Prompter settings, app language | Inside the app on your phone | You |
| Videos you record | Your phone's gallery (album "APrompter"), or inside the app if you turn off *Save takes to the gallery* | You, and any gallery sync you have turned on (Google Photos, iCloud Photos) |
| Backup files you export | Wherever you send them (Files, Drive, email, chat) | You decide |

APrompter has **no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking**. It makes no
network requests of its own.

## Permissions and why

- **Camera and microphone** — only to record videos when you tap *Record*. Nothing is
  streamed or uploaded.
- **Photos / gallery (add)** — to save your takes to the "APrompter" album.
- **Display over other apps** (Android) — only for *Float*, the prompter window you put on top
  of other camera apps. The window is marked secure, so screen recordings and live streams
  show it blank.
- **Notifications** (Android) — Android requires a notification while the floating window is
  running.
- **Face ID / fingerprint** — only if you turn on *App lock*. The check happens on your
  phone; APrompter never sees your biometric data.

## Things outside APrompter's control

- **Device backups.** Android and iOS may include app data (your scripts) in your phone's own
  backup to your Google or Apple account, depending on your phone settings.
- **Gallery sync.** Takes saved to the gallery are synced if Google Photos / iCloud Photos sync
  is on. Keep takes inside the app to avoid this.
- **Clipboard.** *Copy as caption* puts text on the clipboard, which some keyboards sync.

## Children

APrompter does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Delete scripts in the app (and empty *Recently deleted*), or uninstall the app to remove
everything it stored. Videos in your gallery are deleted from the gallery app.
