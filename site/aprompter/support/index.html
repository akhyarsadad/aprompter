Support

Most answers are below. For anything else, write to the developer. You'll get a reply from the person who makes the app.

Email nightowlyyyy@gmail.com Say which phone you use (Android or iPhone) and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Get Unlimited back on a new phone

  1. Sign in to the same Google Play or App Store account you bought with.
  2. In APrompter, open the ⋮ menu on the home screen and tap Upgrade.
  3. Tap Restore purchases.

A purchase made on Google Play can't be moved to an iPhone, and the other way round.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not inside the app.

  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → APrompter.
  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → APrompter.

To be sure you aren't charged again, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You keep Unlimited until then. Refunds are handled by Google or Apple.

Back up your scripts

Your scripts live on your phone. To keep a copy, open the ⋮ menu on the home screen and tap Back up all scripts, then save the file to Drive, Files or email. Restore from a backup in the same menu brings them back.

Bring in a script from another app

Share the note to APrompter from Notes, Keep, Notion or Docs, or tap New script → Paste as new script. You can also open .txt, .md, .docx, .rtf, .odt, .html, Evernote and subtitle files with Import files.

Float doesn't appear (Android)

Float needs permission to display over other apps. If you said no the first time, open Settings → Apps → APrompter → Display over other apps, turn it on, then tap Float again.

Your data

APrompter has no account and never sends your scripts or videos anywhere. To ask for a copy of your purchase records or to have them deleted, email the address above with the order number from your store receipt. The privacy policy explains the details.

Report a bug

Email is best. You can also open an issue on GitHub, but that page is public, so don't post order numbers or other personal details there.
